Defend The Village

Defend the village was my first attempt at an unreal engine game. I was introduced to a plethora of features unreal engine provides like blueprinting and particles and using these, I created a tower defence game. The game I took the most inspiration from was Bloons Tower Defence, however I changed upon the formula by making the player being an active participant in the game.
