Familar Feathers

Genre: Cozy Collectathon
Contribution: Collaborative Project
Software Used: Unreal Engine

Familar Feathers was a group project I contributed to during my second year. I was tasked as a programmer to create the mechanics layed out to me in the design briefs given by the designers.
Over the course of the module I worked on several mechanics which are present in the game. While working on the mechanics, as it had been over a year since I had touched Unreal Engine, I needed to research how several aspects of the blueprinting process to learn how to make the mechanics asked of me.

My Contributions

Picking up plants

I help create the system for picking up the plants around the game world which are used in the game as ingredients for potions. My contribution for this was to create an interface to simplify the process made by another programmer to make the blueprint's functionality more efficient and to ensure future requirements for the pickup system would also be compatiable with it.

Learning how interfaces work was an incredibly interesting experience as seeing the possibilities with this feature opened the doors for what is possible in Unreal Engine for me.

In addition to this, I also created one of the objects which used this new system which later replaced the old one and is used in the final product. The new objects were made for the plants models to be stored in to save the designers time when placing them all around the world.

Quick Time events

The quick time events I created were easily my favourite mechanics I made during the duration of the module, as it was the biggest test of my abilities for several areas of Unreal Engine; I had to use blueprinting and creating UI (User Interfaces) for the mechanics.

The two quick time events were both made for different types of plants that the player could pickup; one plant type used a circle with a green zone which the player had to time a button press to yank the plant out of the group, and the other required mashing two keys back and forth to uproot the plant.

Tutorial Text Box

One of the smaller mechanics I was tasked with near the start of the project was a simple UI element, a tutorial text box that could pop up during the tutorial to explain the mechanics being taught.

The experience I gained from working on the project is invaluable and I am very content with the amount of work that I put into it. I hope to continue working with Unreal Engine in the future to further advance my skill set with the software.
